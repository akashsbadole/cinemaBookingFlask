Pricing Rules Information
How Dynamic Pricing Works:
  • Base prices are set per seat type
  • Multipliers are applied based on show timing
  • Weekend surcharge adds 20% by default
  • Matinee discount reduces by 10% for morning shows
  • Evening surcharge adds 10% for prime time shows
  • Holiday surcharge adds 50% for special days
Seat Type Multipliers:
  • Normal: Base price × 1.0
  • Premium: Base price × 1.5
  • VIP: Base price × 2.0
Minimum Price:

All seats have a minimum price of ₹50.00 regardless of calculations.
